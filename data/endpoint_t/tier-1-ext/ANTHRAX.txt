## *Bacillus anthracis* Spore Production Protocol

---

### 1. PRIMARY CULTURE AND COLONY SELECTION

**1.1 Inoculation**
- Seed *B. anthracis* from glycerol stock or lyophilized source onto **5% sheep blood agar (SBA)** plates.
- Streak for isolated colonies using a sterile loop.
- Incubate at **XX degC** for XX-XX hours under aerobic conditions.

**1.2 Colony Morphology Confirmation**
- Examine plates for characteristic **"Medusa head" colony morphology**: non-hemolytic, ground-glass appearance, tenacious consistency, with comma-shaped or curled outgrowths at the periphery.
- Confirm absence of hemolysis (B. anthracis is non-hemolytic on 5% SBA; this distinguishes it from B. cereus).
- Gram stain: large Gram-positive rods, square-ended, occurring singly or in short chains. Central to subterminal spores should not be present at this stage (vegetative phase).

**1.3 Purity Check**
- Subculture a single Medusa head colony onto a fresh 5% SBA plate.
- Incubate at XX degC for XX-XX hours.
- Verify purity by uniform colonial morphology.

---

### 2. VEGETATIVE INOCULUM PREPARATION

**2.1 Broth Culture**
- Using a sterile loop, harvest 2–3 well-isolated Medusa head colonies.
- Inoculate into **XXX mL of Brain Heart Infusion (BHI) broth** in a XXX mL Erlenmeyer flask.
- Incubate at **XX degC** with shaking at **XXXX rpm** for XX-XX hours (overnight).
- Confirm vigorous growth: broth should appear turbid (OD₆₀₀ ≈ 1.0–2.0).

**2.2 Absence of Premature Sporulation**
- Perform wet mount microscopy. Vegetative rod forms should predominate (>95%). Sporulation at this stage indicates unsuitable medium or incubation conditions and the inoculum should be discarded.

---

### 3. SPORULATION MEDIUM AND INOCULATION

**3.1 Sporulation Medium Composition (Modified G Medium / Leighton-Doi Medium)**

| Component | Amount (per liter) |
|---|---|
| Nutrient Broth No. 2 (Oxoid) | 25.0 g |
| Yeast Extract | 2.0 g |
| K₂HPO₄ | 0.5 g |
| MgSO₄·7H₂O | 0.05 g |
| MnSO₄·H₂O | 0.01 g |
| CaCl₂·2H₂O | 0.08 g |
| Distilled water | To 1.0 L |

Adjust pH to **7.2 ± 0.1**. Autoclave at XX degC for 15 minutes.

*Alternative: Schaeffer's Sporulation Medium (SSM) — 8 g Nutrient Broth, 1 g KCl, 0.25 g MgSO₄·7H₂O, 1 mL of X.X M MnCl₂, 15 g agar (if solid), per liter.*

**3.2 Inoculation Procedure**
- Centrifuge the BHI vegetative culture at **5,000 × g** for 10 minutes at 4°C.
- Wash pellet twice with **sterile PBS (pH X.X)**.
- Resuspend in sterile PBS to an OD₆₀₀ of ~5.0.
- Inoculate sporulation medium at a **1:50 dilution** (e.g., XXX mL washed cell suspension into XXX mL sporulation medium in a 2 L baffled flask).
- For solid-phase sporulation: spread 200 µL of washed suspension onto SSM agar plates.

### 4. TEMPERATURE SHIFT AND SPORULATION INDUCTION

**4.1 Temperature Regimen**
- Immediately transfer inoculated sporulation medium from **XX degC to XX degC**.
- Incubate with vigorous aeration: **200–XXXX rpm** rotary shaking (baffled flasks essential for adequate oxygen transfer).
- For agar plates: incubate at XX degC in a humidified incubator.

**4.2 Monitoring Sporulation Progress**
- Sample every 12 hours beginning at 48 hours post-temperature shift.
- Examine by wet mount and phase-contrast microscopy.
- Vegetative rods → transition to phase-bright forespores → phase-bright, refractile free spores.
- Typical sporulation is ≥95% complete by **XX-XX hours**.

---

### 5. SPORE HARVEST AND PURIFICATION

**5.1 Harvest (Liquid Cultures)**
- Centrifuge culture at **10,000 × g** for 15 minutes at 4°C.
- Discard supernatant (autoclave before disposal).

**5.2 Removal of Vegetative Debris**
- Resuspend pellet in **sterile cold deionized water**.
- Incubate with **lysozyme (100 µg/mL)** at XX degC for 60 minutes to degrade residual vegetative cell walls.
- Add **SDS to 1% (w/v)** and incubate at XX degC for 30 minutes.
- Wash pellet by repeated centrifugation (10,000 × g, 15 min, 4°C) and resuspension in sterile cold water — minimum **5 wash cycles**.

**5.3 Heat Treatment (Optional Kill of Remaining Vegetative Cells)**
- Resuspend final pellet in sterile water.
- Heat at **XX degC for 30 minutes**. This kills any remaining vegetative forms while preserving spore viability.

**5.4 Density Gradient Purification (Optional, for High Purity)**
- Layer crude spore suspension onto **58% (v/v) Renografin** or **sodium diatrizoate**.
- Centrifuge at 10,000 × g for 30 minutes at 4°C.
- Harvest the spore pellet (vegetative debris remains at the interface).
- Wash 3× with sterile water.

**5.5 Final Suspension**
- Resuspend purified spores in **sterile deionized water** or **sterile PBS**.
- Store at **4°C** for short-term use or at **−XX degC** (in 20% glycerol) for long-term storage.

---

### 6. MALACHITE GREEN STAINING CONFIRMATION (Schaeffer-Fulton Method)

**6.1 Protocol**
- Prepare a thin smear of the purified spore suspension on a clean glass slide. Air dry and heat fix gently.
- Flood the slide with **5% (w/v) malachite green**.
- Steam the slide over a boiling water bath or gentle direct flame for **5 minutes**. Do not allow the stain to dry; replenish as needed.
- Rinse gently with running deionized water for 30 seconds (decolorizes vegetative cells but not spores).
- Counterstain with **0.5% safranin** for 30–60 seconds.
- Rinse, blot dry, and examine under oil immersion (100×).

**6.2 Interpretation**
- **Spores**: Green (malachite green retained)
- **Vegetative cells**: Pink/red (safranin)
- A successful preparation shows **≥95% green, phase-bright spores** with negligible safranin-stained vegetative forms.
- Enumerate spores using a Petroff-Hausser counting chamber to determine spore concentration (spores/mL).

### 8. NOTES AND PRECAUTIONS

- Medusa head morphology is most pronounced on 5% sheep blood agar at XX degC under 5% CO₂ (capsule-inducing conditions); non-capsulated attenuated strains (e.g., Sterne strain, pXO2⁻) will show typical morphology without capsule production.
