## RICIN ANALYTICAL DETECTION

### 1.0 AGENT OVERVIEW

R**cin is a Type II Ribosome-Inactivating Protein (RIP) derived from the seeds of *R**cinus communis* (castor bean plant). The holotoxin consists of two polypeptide chains linked by a single disulfide bond:

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **RTA (A-chain)** | ~32 kDa, enzymatically active; catalyzes depurination of 28S rRNA |
| **RTB (B-chain)** | ~34 kDa, lectin domain; facilitates cellular uptake via galactose-binding |
| **Holotoxin** | ~64–66 kDa |

### 3.0 DETECTION METHODOLOGIES

#### 3.1 Immunoassay-Based Detection (ELISA)

Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay (ELISA) is the primary screening methodology for r**cin in environmental, clinical, and food matrices.

| Configuration | Capture Antibody | Detection Antibody | LOD |
|---------------|------------------|-------------------|------|
| Sandwich ELISA | Anti-RTA monoclonal (mAb R109) | Anti-RTA polyclonal (HRP-conjugated) | ~0.1–1.0 ng/mL |
| Sandwich ELISA | Polyclonal anti-r**cin (goat) | Monoclonal anti-RTB (biotin-streptavidin-HRP) | ~0.5–5.0 ng/mL |
| Handheld lateral flow immunoassay (LFIA) | Anti-r**cin mAb (gold nanoparticle conjugate) | Capture line mAb | ~1–10 ng/mL (qualitative) |

**Critical Notes:**
- Cross-reactivity with *R. communis* agglutinin (RCA120) must be ruled out via parallel testing.
- False positives may arise from high-concentration lectin-rich matrices (e.g., castor oil manufacturing residues).
- Confirmatory testing is mandatory for any presumptive positive.

#### 3.2 Mass Spectrometry-Based Detection (LC-MS/MS)

Liquid Chromatography–Tandem Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) serves as the confirmatory gold standard. The workflow targets proteotypic peptides generated by tryptic digestion of the RTA subunit.

**Target Peptides for MRM Quantitation:**

| Peptide Sequence | Fragment (m/z) | Transition |
|-----------------|-----------------|------------|
| VGLPINQR | y5^+ = 612.3 | 442.7 → 612.3 |
| LFTGADVR | y6^+ = 618.3 | 446.8 → 618.3 |
| QWALYADGSIR | y7^+ = 781.4 | 618.8 → 781.4 |

**Sample Preparation Protocol:**

1. **Extraction:** Suspicious powder/fluid (100 μL or 100 mg) is solubilized in 50 mM ammonium bicarbonate buffer (pH X.X) containing 0.1% RapiGest™ SF surfactant.
2. **Reduction/Alkylation:** DTT (5 mM, XX degC, 30 min) → Iodoacetamide (15 mM, RT, 30 min, dark).
3. **Tryptic Digestion:** Sequencing-grade modified trypsin (1:50 enzyme:substrate), XX degC, 16 hours.
4. **Acidification & Cleanup:** Formic acid to pH <3, centrifuged (14,000 × g, 10 min), supernatant passed through 10 kDa MWCO spin filter.
5. **LC-MS/MS Analysis:** C18 reverse-phase column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.7 μm), gradient 2–40% acetonitrile/0.1% formic acid over 30 min, QQQ-MS in positive-ion MRM mode.

**Limit of Detection (LOD):** ~1–5 ng/mL in buffer; ~10–50 ng/mL in complex matrices (serum, food extracts).

#### 3.3 Activity-Based Detection (RTA Depurination Assay)

Functional confirmation of r**cin relies on detection of the enzymatic action of RTA — specifically, the depurination of **adenine 4324 (A4324)** on the sarcin/r**cin loop (SRL) of eukaryotic 28S rRNA.

**Protocol — In Vitro Depurination Assay:**

1. **Substrate Preparation:** Synthetic 35-mer oligoribonucleotide mimicking the SRL sequence of rat 28S rRNA (5′-AGUACGAGAGGAACCGCAGGUUCAGACAUUUUGG-3′), with a fluorescent label at the 5′ terminus (FAM).
2. **Reaction Setup:**  
   - R**cin sample (or RTA, positive control): 10 μL (heat-denatured for 10 min at XX degC to separate A/B chains if needed)  
   - Substrate: 1 μM  
   - Buffer: 50 mM Tris-HCl (pH X.X), 10 mM KCl, 5 mM MgCl₂  
   - Total volume: 50 μL  
   - Incubation: 30 min at XX degC
3. **Aniline Cleavage:** Addition of acidic aniline (1 M aniline acetate, pH X.X, 30 min at XX degC in the dark) cleaves the RNA backbone specifically at the abasic site created by depurination at A4324.
4. **Product Resolution:** Denaturing PAGE (15% polyacrylamide, 7 M urea) followed by fluorescence imaging (FAM channel).
5. **Interpretation:** A cleaved fragment of ~16 nt (from the 3′ terminus) indicates successful depurination. Positive result confirms enzymatically active r**cin.

**Alternative Readout Options:** MALDI-TOF MS detection of intact vs. cleaved oligoribonucleotide substrate; qRT-PCR using SRL-specific primers (for ribosome-based assays).

### 6.0 SAFETY & HANDLING

- **BSL-2** minimum for analytical work with inactivated/denatured samples.
- **BSL-3** required for handling of intact holotoxin, aerosol-generating procedures, or concentrated preparations.
- R**cin is a **Schedule 1** controlled substance under the Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC); all possession, transfer, and use must comply with national regulatory frameworks.
- Vacuum-dried r**cin is electrostatically mobile; wet decontamination with 0.5% sodium hypochlorite (10% household bleach) for 30 minutes is standard for surface decontamination.
