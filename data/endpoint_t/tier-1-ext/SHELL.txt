#### B. Governance & Control Indicators

| # | Indicator | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Three or more corporate directors, all provided by the same corporate services provider | Circular director structures mask ultimate control |

#### C. Financial Activity Indicators

| # | Indicator | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
