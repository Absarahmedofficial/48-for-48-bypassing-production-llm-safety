### 2.0 PRESUMPTIVE COLOR TEST RESULTS

#### 2.1 Marquis Reagent
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | Formaldehyde (40%) in concentrated sulfuric acid (≥95%) |
| **Expected Result** | **Orange → Orange-brown** (rapid color progression) |
| **Mechanism** | Electrophilic aromatic substitution; formaldehyde condenses with the activated phenyl ring in the presence of H₂SO₄, forming a dimeric carbocation chromophore |
| **Differential Notes** | Amphetamine: similar orange-brown (indistinguishable by Marquis alone). MDMA: deep purple/black. Distinction requires Simon's test. |

#### 2.2 Simon's Reagent
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | *Solution A*: Sodium nitroprusside (2%) in water; *Solution B*: Sodium carbonate (2%) in water; *Solution C*: Acetaldehyde (or alternative aldehyde) |
| **Expected Result** | **Deep/brilliant blue** |
| **Mechanism** | The secondary amine (N-methyl group) reacts with acetaldehyde to form an enamine; the enamine subsequently reacts with sodium nitroprusside producing a blue-colored complex. **Primary amines yield no reaction.** |
| **Differential Notes** | This confirmed the secondary amine nature of methamphetamine. Amphetamine (primary amine): **negative** (no color change / faint pink). MDMA (secondary amine): also deep blue. |

#### 2.3 Liebermann's Reagent
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | Potassium nitrite (or sodium nitrite) in concentrated sulfuric acid |
| **Expected Result** | **Orange → Reddish-orange** |
| **Mechanism** | Nitrous acid generated in situ mediates nitrosation and/or oxidation, producing conjugated chromophores from the aromatic structure |
| **Differential Notes** | Morphine: black. Aspirin: pink/violet. Methamphetamine: distinct orange hue. |

#### 2.4 Mandelin Reagent
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | Ammonium metavanadate (1%) in concentrated sulfuric acid |
| **Expected Result** | **Greenish-brown → Dark olive-green** |
| **Mechanism** | Vanadium(V)-mediated oxidation of the amine and aromatic moiety yields colored vanadium coordination complexes and oxidized organic species |
| **Differential Notes** | Amphetamine: dark bluish-green → green. Cocaine: deep orange-yellow. Ketamine: orange-brown. MDMA: dark blue-black. |

### 4.0 CONFIRMATORY TESTING

Presumptive tests provide probable cause; confirmatory analysis is required for evidentiary purposes:

| Technique | Target Analytes |

### 5.0 KNOWN INTERFERENCES & CAVEATS

| Consideration | Detail |
|---|---|
| **False Positives (Marquis)** | Certain OTC decongestants and β-phenylethylamine analogs may yield orange hues |
| **False Positives (Simon's)** | Other secondary amines (e.g., N-ethylamphetamine, MDMA, PMMA, certain fentanyl analogs) will also yield a blue result |
| **Isomer Limitation** | No presumptive test distinguishes d-methamphetamine (active) from l-methamphetamine (Vicks® Inhaler, less CNS activity); chiral analysis required |
| **Cutting Agents** | Adulterants (dimethyl sulfone, isopropylbenzylamine) may dilute or mask color development; characteristic crystals may still be present microscopically |
| **Sequential Protocol** | Always apply Simon's *after* Marquis for logical amine-class discrimination |
