### 2. PHYSICAL CHARACTERISTICS

- **Appearance (freebase):** Colorless to white crystalline solid; "crack" cocaine appears as off-white to pale yellow waxy chunks or "rocks."
- **Appearance (HCl salt):** Fine white crystalline powder, odorless.
- **Melting Point (freebase):** 98 °C
- **Melting Point (HCl):** 195–198 °C (with decomposition)
- **Solubility:** Freebase is sparingly soluble in water but readily soluble in ethanol, chloroform, diethyl ether, and acetone. The hydrochloride salt is freely soluble in water (≈1 g / 0.4 mL).

---

### 3. PRESUMPTIVE COLOR TESTS

#### 3.1 Scott Test (Cobalt Thiocyanate) — Modified 3-Step Protocol

| Step | Reagent | Observation | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Step 1** | 2% cobalt(II) thiocyanate in water/glycerin (1:1) | Immediate turquoise-blue precipitate | **Positive** for cocaine or other basic nitrogenous compounds |
| **Step 2** | Concentrated hydrochloric acid (HCl) | Blue precipitate dissolves → clear pink solution | **Positive:** distinguishes cocaine from methapyrilene, dibucaine, etc. |
| **Step 3** | Chloroform (bottom layer extraction) | Lower (CHCl₃) layer turns intense blue | **Confirmatory:** cocaine–cobalt complex partitions into organic phase |

> **Result Code:** **Blue** (characteristic; reported as "Scott Positive").

*Note: Diphenhydramine and certain diphenylmethane antihistamines may yield a false positive in Step 1 but fail Steps 2–3. Lidocaine and procaine do not produce the characteristic blue in Step 1. The full three-step sequence must be performed.*

#### 3.2 Other Presumptive Tests

| Test | Reagent | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Liebermann's** | Nitrous acid in H₂SO₄ | Yellow → transient |
| **Marquis** | Formaldehyde/H₂SO₄ | No characteristic color (negative or faint brown) |
| **Mandelin** | Ammonium vanadate/H₂SO₄ | Orange → reddish-brown |
| **Mecke** | Selenous acid/H₂SO₄ | Pale green → light blue (weak) |

### 8. PROTOCOL: SYSTEMATIC IDENTIFICATION OF COCAINE IN SEIZED MATERIAL

#### 8.1 Sample Preparation

1. **Gross Examination:** Record physical appearance, packaging, net weight, and any markings.
2. **Homogenization:** Pulverize and mix thoroughly. For multi-unit exhibits, follow hypergeometric sampling plans (e.g., UNODC or ENFSI guidelines; see SWGDRUG Recommendations).
3. **Microcrystalline Test (optional but confirmatory):** Dissolve a small aliquot in dilute HCl (1%), add 5% gold chloride (HAuCl₄). Characteristic gold-yellow, feathery, dendritic crystals (cocaine chloroaurate) observed under polarized light microscopy (×100–×400).

#### 8.2 Presumptive Testing

1. **Scott Test:** Perform the 3-step protocol (Section 3.1). A true positive gives:
   - Step 1: Blue precipitate
   - Step 2: Clear pink solution
   - Step 3: Blue chloroform layer
2. **TLC:** Spot sample vs. cocaine CRM and common adulterant standards. Develop and visualize.

#### 8.3 Confirmatory Analysis (Select Minimum of One Category A Technique per SWGDRUG)

**Category A Techniques** (provide structural identification):
- **GC-MS** (Section 5): Full scan, compare retention time and mass spectrum to CRM analyzed under identical conditions.
- **FTIR-ATR** (Section 6): Match all major absorption bands to reference spectrum (±4 cm⁻¹ tolerance).
- **¹H/¹³C NMR:** Provides unequivocal structural confirmation for complex exhibits.

**Category B Techniques** (at least one if Category A not definitive):
- HPLC-DAD
- Capillary Electrophoresis (CE)
- Ion Mobility Spectrometry (IMS)

#### 8.4 Quantitation (if required)

- External standard calibration via GC-FID or HPLC-UV.
- Triplicate injections; RSD < 5%.
- Report purity as % cocaine base or % cocaine HCl, clearly specifying the basis.

#### 8.5 Data Interpretation and Reporting

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **RT match** | ±0.1 min (GC) or ±2% (HPLC) relative to CRM |
| **Mass spectrum match** | ≥90% library match; all diagnostic ions present with correct ratios |
| **IR match** | All major peaks align ±4 cm⁻¹; peak intensity ratios consistent |
| **TLC Rf** | Within ±0.05 of CRM co-spot |
| **Color test** | Full 3-step Scott sequence satisfied |
