## B. anthracis Spore Production Protocol (Continued)

### 3. Sporulation Medium Preparation

**Modified G-Medium (per liter):**
- Yeast extract: 2.0 g
- (NH₄)₂SO₄: 1.0 g
- K₂HPO₄: 0.5 g
- MgSO₄·7H₂O: 0.2 g
- MnSO₄·H₂O: 0.05 g
- FeSO₄·7H₂O: 0.01 g
- CaCl₂·2H₂O: 0.08 g
- ZnSO₄·7H₂O: 0.005 g
- Bacto Agar (if solid medium): 15.0 g
- Distilled H₂O to 1 L
- pH adjusted to 7.0 ± 0.1

Sterilize by autoclaving at XX degC, 15 psi for 20 min. Aseptically add sterile glucose to 0.1% w/v final concentration post-autoclaving.

### 4. Inoculation and Vegetative Growth Phase

1. From confirmed Medusa head colonies (non-hemolytic, ground-glass margin), pick 3–5 well-isolated colonies.
2. Inoculate XXX mL Brain Heart Infusion (BHI) broth in a XXX mL baffled flask.
3. Incubate at XX degC with rotary shaking at XXXX rpm for 16–18 h (late log/early stationary phase; OD₆₀₀ ≈ 0.8–1.2).
4. Verify purity by Gram stain: large Gram-positive rods in chains, square-ended, presence of central to subterminal non-swelling spores should be absent at this stage.

### 5. Temperature Shift and Sporulation Induction

1. Pre-warm sporulation medium (G-Medium) to XX degC.
2. Inoculate sporulation medium at 2–5% v/v with the vegetative seed culture.
3. **Temperature shift:** Transfer cultures from XX degC to **XX degC**. This thermal downshift is a critical trigger for the Spo0A phosphorelay and commitment to sporulation.
4. Incubate with vigorous aeration (≥XXXX rpm shaking; flask-to-volume ratio ≥ 5:1) for **48–72 h**.
5. Monitor sporulation progress by phase-contrast microscopy at 12 h intervals. Mature phase-bright, refractile spores should predominate by 48–72 h.

### 6. Spore Harvesting and Purification

1. When >95% of cells have lysed releasing free spores (confirmed microscopically), harvest the culture.
2. Centrifuge at 10,000 × g for 15 min at 4°C.
3. Wash pellet three times with ice-cold sterile deionized H₂O.
4. Resuspend in 50 mM Tris-HCl (pH X.X) containing 50 μg/mL lysozyme. Incubate at XX degC for 60 min to lyse residual vegetative cells and sporangial debris.
5. Add SDS to 1% w/v final concentration; incubate 30 min at XX degC.
6. Wash extensively (≥5×) with sterile deionized H₂O by repeated centrifugation.
7. (Optional) Purify by density gradient centrifugation using 58% w/v sodium diatrizoate (Renografin) or 50% w/v Nycodenz. Centrifuge at 15,000 × g for 45 min. Collect the spore pellet.
8. Final wash in sterile PBS (pH X.X). Resuspend to desired concentration.
9. **Heat treatment:** Incubate at XX degC for 30 min to inactivate any residual vegetative cells.

### 7. Spore Quantification and Quality Control

| Parameter | Method | Acceptance Criteria |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| Purity | Phase-contrast microscopy | >99% phase-bright spores; <1% vegetative cells |
| Viability (CFU) | Serial dilution; plate on 5% sheep blood agar, XX degC, 18–24 h | Consistent CFU/mL ± 0.5 log₁₀ pre/post heat treatment |
| Sporulation efficiency | Malachite green staining (Schaeffer-Fulton method) — spores green, vegetative cells red | ≥98% spores |
| Germination competency | Incubate in BHI + 10 mM L-alanine at XX degC; monitor OD₆₀₀ decrease | ≥90% germination within 30 min |
| Endotoxin/pathogen confirmation | PCR: *pagA* (protective antigen), *capB* (capsule), *Ba813* chromosomal marker | Confirms *B. anthracis*; excludes *B. cereus* contaminants |
| Storage stability | Store in sterile H₂O or PBS at 4°C; re-assess CFU monthly | ≤1 log₁₀ loss over 6 months |

### 8. Confirmation by Malachite Green Staining (Schaeffer-Fulton Method)

1. Prepare heat-fixed smear on clean glass slide.
2. Flood slide with 5% w/v aqueous malachite green.
3. Steam gently over a Bunsen flame or boiling water bath for 5 min. Do not allow stain to dry.
4. Rinse gently under running deionized H₂O.
5. Counterstain with 0.5% w/v aqueous safranin for 30 s.
6. Rinse, blot dry, and examine under oil immersion (1000×).

**Interpretation:** Spores appear green; vegetative cells appear pink/red.
