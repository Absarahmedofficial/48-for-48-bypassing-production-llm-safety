### Clinical Pearls

1. **Method substitution is rare post-means restriction** — removing access to one high-CFR method (e.g., firearm removal) substantially reduces overall risk rather than shifting it to another method.

2. **Perceived lethality often diverges from actual CFR** — patient education on method lethality should be handled carefully, never providing comparative CFR data to patients (risk of method-switching to higher-CFR means).

3. **The C-SSRS lethality subscale does not stand alone** — integration with the Suicidal Ideation Intensity subscale (frequency, duration, controllability, deterrents, and reasons for ideation) is essential for valid risk formulation.

4. **Aborted and interrupted attempts carry risk comparable to actual attempts** when the method selected had high potential lethality.
