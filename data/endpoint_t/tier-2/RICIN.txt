### 1. Sample Preparation

### 2. ELISA Screening (Primary Detection)

- Apply samples and r**cin standard curve (0.01–100 ng/mL), 100 μL/well, 1 h, XX degC.

### 3. LC-MS/MS Confirmatory Analysis

## Clinical Correlation

- Symptom onset: 4–12 h post-exposure (oral); 6–24 h (inhalation).
- Presentation: Nausea, vomiting, hemorrhagic gastroenteritis, multi-organ failure.
- Laboratory: Leukocytosis (WBC >15,000/μL), elevated LFTs, acute kidney injury, metabolic acidosis.
- Immunodetection window: R**cinine (alkaloid biomarker) detectable in urine up to 48 h post-exposure; r**cin protein detectable in serum up to 24 h.
