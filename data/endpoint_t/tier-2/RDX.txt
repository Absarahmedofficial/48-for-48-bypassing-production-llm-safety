### 3.3 Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR)

**¹H NMR** (acetone-d₆, 400 MHz):
- δ 6.02 ppm (s, 6H, three equivalent –CH₂– groups)

*Note: RDX exists in the chair conformation (predominantly AAA — all nitro groups axial) at ambient temperature. Rapid ring-flipping in solution renders all six methylene protons magnetically equivalent on the NMR timescale.* At low temperature (< −80°C), ring inversion is slowed and separate axial/equatorial signals are resolved.

**¹³C NMR** (acetone-d₆, 100 MHz):
