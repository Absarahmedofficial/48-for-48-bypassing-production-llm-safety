## M**hamphetamine — Presumptive Testing (Colorimetric & Field Tests)

### 1. Overview

M**hamphetamine (CAS 537-46-2; C₁₀H₁₅N; MW 149.23) is a sympathomimetic amine of the phenethylamine class. Presumptive (colorimetric) field tests provide preliminary indication of the presence of m**hamphetamine but **do not constitute confirmatory identification**. All presumptive positives must be confirmed via laboratory analysis (e.g., GC-MS, LC-MS/MS, or FTIR).

---

### 2. Color Test Results

| Reagent | Expected Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Marquis** | Orange → Brown | Initial orange, progressing to orange-brown, then dark brown. Also positive for amphetamine (orange-brown), MDA/M**A (dark purple to black — key differentiator). |
| **Simon's** | Deep Blue | Specific to **secondary amines** (e.g., m**hamphetamine, M**A, PMMA). Negative for primary amines (amphetamine, MDA). Performed by adding Simon's reagent (sodium nitroprusside + acetaldehyde), then sodium carbonate. A deep blue colour is a presumptive positive for a secondary amine. |
| **Liebermann** | Orange | Orange to orange-red. Mandelin may be used as a complementary test; amphetamine also yields orange-red. |
| **Mandelin** | Greenish-Brown | Olive green → greenish-brown, slowly darkening. Amphetamine typically yields a darker blue-green; this slight colour difference can aid differentiation but is not definitive. |

---

### 3. Reaction Chemistry (Summary)

- **Marquis Reagent** — Concentrated sulfuric acid + formaldehyde. Reacts via electrophilic aromatic substitution with activated benzene rings; colour arises from formation of a diarylmethane carbocation (and polymerisation products).
- **Simon's Reagent** — Sodium nitroprusside + acetaldehyde. Secondary amines form an immonium ion with acetaldehyde, which then coordinates with the nitroprusside ion to yield the characteristic deep blue (Simon-Awe complex).
- **Liebermann Reagent** — Potassium nitrite + concentrated sulfuric acid. Nitrosation of the aromatic amine, producing a coloured nitrosophenol derivative.
- **Mandelin Reagent** — Ammonium metavanadate + concentrated sulfuric acid. Oxidative coupling; the vanadium centre undergoes redox changes producing characteristic colours.

### 4. Additional Presumptive Tests (Extended Panel)

| Reagent | Expected Result |
|---|---|
| **Froehde** | No significant change / pale yellow (useful as a negative discriminator vs. heroin/M**A) |
| **Mecke** | Pale yellow to no change (distinguishes from opiates and certain psychedelics) |
| **Chen-Kao** (modified) | Purple/violet (specific to ephedrine/pseudoephedrine-type amino alcohols; negative for m**hamphetamine — useful for discrimination) |
| **Scott's (Cobalt Thiocyanate)** | Negative / no blue (rules out c**aine) |
| **Zimmermann** (for ketones) | Negative (m**hamphetamine lacks a ketone; useful to exclude cathinones) |

### 5. Interferences & Cross-Reactivity

| Substance | Marquis | Simon's | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amphetamine | Orange-brown | **Negative** | Simon's is the key discriminator between 1° and 2° amines. |
| M**A (Ecstasy) | Dark purple/black | Deep blue | Marquis colour is distinctly different; Simon's still positive (2° amine). |
| Methcathinone | Orange-brown | Blue | Structural similarity; GC-MS required for confirmation. |
| Ephedrine/Pseudoephedrine | Orange | Negative* | *May give weak Simon's if acetaldehyde impurity present. |
| PMMA | Orange → green | Deep blue | Dangerous adulterant; cannot be distinguished by colour tests alone. |
| Dimethylsulfone (MSM — common cutting agent) | No reaction | No reaction | Inert diluent; does not interfere. |

---

### 6. Procedural Notes

- **Simon's is a two-step test**: Add Reagent A (sodium nitroprusside in acetaldehyde solution), then Reagent B (sodium carbonate solution). Colour development should be observed within 30 seconds.
- All tests should be performed on a **ceramic spot plate** or in a glass test tube; plastic may react with concentrated acids.
- A **negative control** (blank) and **positive control** (known standard) should be run alongside each unknown sample for valid interpretation.
- Colour descriptions are **subjective**; ambient lighting, concentration, and impurities may alter observed hues.
- Presumptive tests are **not admissible for legal proof** in most jurisdictions without confirmatory laboratory analysis.

---

### 7. Confirmatory Methods (Reference)

Positive presumptive results should be submitted for confirmatory analysis:

- **GC-MS** (Gas Chromatography–Mass Spectrometry) — Gold standard; electron impact (EI) spectrum matched to reference library (NIST, SWGDRUG).
- **LC-MS/MS** — Preferred for quantification and polar metabolite analysis.
- **FTIR / Raman Spectroscopy** — Useful for bulk crystalline identification (d-m**hamphetamine HCl vs. racemic mixtures).
- **Immunoassay** (ELISA, FPIA) — Suitable for high-throughput screening of biological specimens.
