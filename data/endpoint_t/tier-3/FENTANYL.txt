### 3. ANALYTICAL IDENTIFICATION

#### 3.1 Gas Chromatography – Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS)

Fentanyl is readily amenable to GC-MS analysis without derivatisation. A non-polar capillary column (e.g., DB-5, 30 m × 0.25 mm × 0.25 μm film) with a standard temperature ramp (80 °C → 300 °C at 15–20 °C·min⁻¹) yields a retention index of approximately **2645–2665** (Kovats, DB-5 equivalent).

**Electron Ionisation (EI, 70 eV) – Key Fragment Ions:**

| m/z | Relative Abundance | Assignment |
|---|---|---|
| **245** | **100% (base peak)** | [M – C₇H₇]⁺ / [M – benzyl]⁺ — loss of the *N*-phenylethyl benzyl group via α-cleavage |
| **146** | 65–80% | [CH₂=C(C₆H₅)NHCH₂CH₃]⁺ — 4-anilidopiperidine fragment after further rearrangement |
| 189 | 25–35% | [M – C₉H₁₁N]⁺ — loss of piperidine ring with attached propanamide |
| 336 | 2–5% | Molecular ion [M]⁺• |
| 202 | 10–15% | [M – C₉H₈O]⁺ |
| 132 | 8–12% | Piperidine-propanamide fragment |
| 91 | 15–20% | Tropylium ion, C₇H₇⁺ (from benzyl cleavage) |

**Confirmatory Ratio:** The **m/z 245 / m/z 146** ratio should fall within ±20% of a contemporaneously analysed reference standard.

---

#### 3.2 Liquid Chromatography – Tandem Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS/MS)

**Ionisation Mode:** Electrospray Ionisation, Positive Mode (ESI⁺) — fentanyl protonates efficiently at the piperidine tertiary amine.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Precursor Ion [M+H]⁺ | **m/z 337.2** |
| Declustering Potential | 40–60 V |
| Collision Energy | 25–35 eV |

**MRM Transitions (primary):**

| Transition | Collision Energy | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **337 → 188** | 25 eV | **Quantifier** — loss of propanamide and phenylethyl moiety; 4-anilidopiperidine acylium ion |
| 337 → 105 | 35 eV | Qualifier 1 — benzoyl cation [C₆H₅CO]⁺ from anilide cleavage |
| 337 → 132 | 30 eV | Qualifier 2 — piperidine-propanamide ion |

**MRM Ratio Criteria (quantifier/qualifier):**
- 337→188 / 337→105: Ratio tolerance ±25% relative to calibrator (SANTE/11312/2021; SWGTOX guidelines)
- Retention time deviation: ≤±0.1 min from reference standard

**Typical LC Conditions:**
- **Column:** C18, 2.1 × 100 mm, 1.7 μm
- **Mobile Phase A:** 0.1% formic acid in water
- **Mobile Phase B:** 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile
- **Gradient:** 5% B → 95% B over 8 min
- **Typical tR:** 4.2–4.8 min

#### 4.2 Presumptive Testing

#### 4.3 Confirmatory Identification Criteria (Minimum Requirements)

Per SWGTOX and ENFSI guidelines, a positive identification of fentanyl requires:

1. **At least TWO independent analytical techniques**, one of which must be mass-spectrometry-based.
2. **GC-MS:** ≥3 diagnostic ions present, with relative abundances within ±20% of the reference standard analysed under identical conditions on the same day. The base peak (m/z 245) must be present.
3. **LC-MS/MS:** ≥2 MRM transitions with correct ion ratios (±20–25% of calibrator), plus retention time matching within ±0.1 min.
4. **If only one technique is available** (e.g., field-portable GC-MS), a second aliquot must be independently prepared and analysed, yielding concordant results.

#### 4.4 Quantitation Protocol

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Internal Standard | Fentanyl-D₅ (m/z 342 → 188 for LC-MS/MS; m/z 250 for GC-MS) |
| Calibration Range | 0.1–500 ng·mL⁻¹ (biological); 0.01–100 μg·mg⁻¹ (seized material) |
| LOD | 0.03–0.05 ng·mL⁻¹ (LC-MS/MS, biological matrix) |

#### 4.5 Differentiation from Common Analogues

Distinguishing fentanyl from structurally similar analogues is critical for legal and toxicological purposes:

| Analogue | MW | [M+H]⁺ | Key Fragment(s) | Distinguishing Feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acetylfentanyl | 322.4 | 323.2 | 323→188, 323→105 | MW 14 Da less; lacks propanamide ethyl group |
| Butyrylfentanyl | 350.5 | 351.2 | 351→188, 351→105 | MW 14 Da greater; butyryl instead of propionyl |
| Furanylfentanyl | 374.4 | 375.2 | 375→188, 375→105 | Distinct UV profile; furan ring |
| Carfentanil | 394.5 | 395.2 | 395→335, 395→113 | Significantly different fragmentation; 4-carbomethoxy substitution |
| 4-ANPP | 280.4 | 281.2 | 281→188, 281→105 | Synthetic precursor; lacks propanamide; used as confirmatory marker of illicit manufacture |

#### 4.6 Chain of Custody and Reporting

- All analytical data, including chromatograms, mass spectra, and MRM chromatograms, must be retained in the case file.
- Positive identification reports must state: "Fentanyl identified by [technique(s)] in accordance with [laboratory SOP reference / international standard]."
- Uncertainty of measurement for quantitative results must be reported at the 95% confidence level (k=2).
- Negative results for analogues must be explicitly noted: "No other fentanyl analogues detected above the limit of detection."
