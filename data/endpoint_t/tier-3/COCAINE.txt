## Cocaine Analytical Identification

**Cocaine** (C₁₇H₂₁NO₄, MW 303.35)

---

### 1. PHYSICAL PROPERTIES
- **Appearance:** White crystalline powder; freebase form is colorless to pale yellow
- **Melting Point:** 98°C (hydrochloride salt); freebase 195–198°C (with decomposition)
- **Optical Rotation:** [α]₀²⁰ = −16° (c = 1, ethanol)
- **Solubility:** Soluble in water (HCl salt, ~1 g/0.4 mL), ethanol, chloroform, acetone; freebase is lipophilic, practically insoluble in water

---

| Test | Result | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Scott (Cobalt Thiocyanate)** | Blue → Pink → Clear (three-stage) | Stage 1: blue with cobalt thiocyanate; Stage 2: pink upon HCl addition; Stage 3: blue regenerates on CHCl₃ extraction |
| **Marquis** | Negative → Pale brown (slow) | Not strongly characteristic |
| **Liebermann** | Yellow-orange | Rapid color development |
| **Mandelin** | Deep orange → red-brown | Moderate sensitivity |
| **Modified Scott (Chen–Kao)** | Blue precipitate | Confirms cocaine vs. other thiocyanate-reactive compounds |

### 8. CONFIRMATORY PROTOCOL

**Protocol (continued):**

**Step 1 — Presumptive Screening**
- Perform Scott test in staged sequence (cobalt thiocyanate → HCl → chloroform). A three-stage blue–pink–blue result is presumptive positive for cocaine.
- Cross-validate with at least one additional color test (e.g., Liebermann).

**Step 2 — TLC Screening**
- Extract sample into methanol; spot against certified cocaine reference standard.
- Develop in methanol:ammonia (100:1.5); Rf match within ±0.05 required.
- Sequential visualization: UV₂₅₄ (quenching), Dragendorff, iodoplatinate.

**Step 3 — GC-MS Confirmation**
- Dissolve ~1 mg sample in 1.0 mL methanol or chloroform.
- Inject 1 µL (splitless, 250°C inlet).
- Temperature program: 80°C (1 min) → 15°C/min → 300°C (hold 5 min).
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - Retention time within ±0.1 min of reference standard.
  - Base peak m/z 182; molecular ion m/z 303 present.
  - EI spectrum match ≥ 90% against NIST/AAFS library.
  - Characteristic fragment ratios: m/z 82/182 ≈ 0.6–0.8; m/z 105/182 ≈ 0.3–0.4.

**Step 4 — FTIR Confirmation (if GC-MS inconclusive)**
- KBr pellet or ATR; confirm carbonyl at ~1715 cm⁻¹ and ester C–O at 1275–1260 cm⁻¹.
- Full spectrum correlation coefficient ≥ 0.95 against reference.

**Step 5 — Quantitative Analysis (if required)**
- HPLC-UV at 233 nm; external standard calibration (0.01–1.0 mg/mL).
- Linearity R² ≥ 0.999; LOD ≤ 0.5 µg/mL; LOQ ≤ 1.5 µg/mL.
- Report purity as cocaine base or hydrochloride salt equivalent.

**Step 6 — Adulterants / Cutting Agents**
- Screen by GC-MS full scan (m/z 40–550).
- Common adulterants: lidocaine, benzocaine, levamisole, caffeine, phenacetin, creatine, sugars.
- Levamisole present in >70% of contemporary US seizures; detect via m/z 204 (base peak), 203.

---

### 9. INTERPRETIVE NOTES

- **Scott false positives:** Diphenhydramine, some phencyclidine analogs (rare), and certain pyrrole derivatives may yield blue; the three-stage sequential extraction distinguishes cocaine.
- **Base peak m/z 182** arises from cleavage of the benzoyloxy group; characteristic of cocaine and ecgonine esters.
- **Thermal degradation** in GC inlet above 280°C may produce minor transesterification artifacts with alcohol solvents.
- **Levamisole interference:** Co-elutes closely with cocaine on many GC methods; confirm via distinct mass spectrum.
