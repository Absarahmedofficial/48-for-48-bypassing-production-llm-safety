### 9. SYNTHESIS OVERVIEW (Informational)

R**X is produced via nitrolysis of hexamethylenetetramine (hexamine) with concentrated nitric acid (Bachmann, Woolwich, or K-processes). The overall reaction:

> C₆H₁₂N₄ + 4 HNO₃ → C₃H₆N₆O₆ + 3 CH₂O + NH₄NO₃ + H₂O

Purification is achieved by recrystallization from acetone, cyclohexanone, or γ-butyrolactone.
