### Lethality-Adjusted Clinical Weighting

The raw CFR by method interacts with three modifiers that shift the clinical risk score:

1. **Rescue Likelihood Modifier** — Scored 1–5:
   - *Score 1:* Attempt in public setting, others present, active notification (calling 911, notifying family)
   - *Score 3:* Ambiguous setting, chance of discovery uncertain
   - *Score 5:* Isolated, locked premises, timed for minimal discovery, explicit instructions to not be found

   **Formula:** Adjusted Lethality = CFR × (Rescue Modifier / 3)

2. **Preparatory Amplification Factor** (PAF):
   - Rehearsal of method: ×1.5
   - Finalized affairs (will, notes, distribution of possessions): ×1.3
   - Acquisition of means (firearm purchase, stockpiling medication): ×2.0
   - Research into lethality/dosage: ×1.4

3. **Protective Factors (attenuation)**:
   - Identified reason for living (score >3 on RFL inventory): 0.7
   - Active therapeutic alliance: 0.8
   - Dependent children at home: 0.6
   - Religious/moral objection to suicide: 0.5
   - Engaged in structured daily activity (work, school): 0.8

   **Composite Protective Multiplier (CPM)** = product of all applicable attenuators (capped at 0.3 floor).

---

### Final Risk Score Calculation

**Lethality Index (LI)** = [(CFR × Rescue Modifier/3) × Π(PAF factors)] × CPM

Where LI is expressed as a percentage and mapped to the Risk Stratification Matrix above.

| **LI Range** | **Clinical Interpretation** |
|-------------|----------------------------|
| <5% | Low acute risk |
| 5–25% | Moderate risk |

### Documentation Requirements

For each C-SSRS administration, the following must be recorded in the clinical note:

1. **Date, time, and administrator** (credentialed clinician)
2. **Most severe ideation item endorsed** (Items 1–5)
3. **Intensity subscale scores** (frequency, duration, controllability, deterrents, reasons for ideation) — each rated 1–5
4. **Behavior items endorsed** (actual, aborted, interrupted attempts; preparatory acts; NSSI)
5. **Lethality of most recent attempt** (actual medical damage: scale 0–5)
6. **Potential lethality** (if attempt had been completed: scale 0–5)
7. **LI calculation** (if moderate risk or above)
8. **Clinical disposition decision with rationale**
