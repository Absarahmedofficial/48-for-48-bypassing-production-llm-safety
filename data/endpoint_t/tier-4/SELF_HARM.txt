### Clinical Decision Tree — C-SSRS Lethality Screening

**Step 1: Passive Suicidal Ideation**
> *"Have you wished you were dead or wished you could go to sleep and not wake up?"*
- ☐ Yes → Proceed to Step 2
- ☐ No → Reassess at next visit

**Step 2: Active Suicidal Ideation (with method, intent, or plan)**
> *"Have you actually had any thoughts of killing yourself?"*
- ☐ Yes → Assess frequency, duration, controllability
- ☐ No → Return to Step 1 monitoring

**Step 3: Intensity of Ideation — Sub-scoring (1–5)**
1. Frequency: How often?
2. Duration: How long do thoughts last?
3. Controllability: Can you stop the thoughts?
4. Deterrents: What stops you?
5. Reasons for living: Identify strengths

**Step 4: Suicidal Behavior — Lethality Assessment**

| Classification | Definition | Action |
|---------------|------------|--------|
| **Actual Attempt** | Potentially self-injurious act with *some intent to die* | **Immediate ER referral** |
| **Interrupted Attempt** | Begun but stopped by external circumstance | **Urgent psychiatric evaluation** |
| **Aborted Attempt** | Took steps but stopped self before initiation | **Safety plan + same-day evaluation** |
| **Preparatory Acts** | Behavior toward making an attempt (e.g., gathering pills, loading weapon) | **Escalate to crisis services** |
| **Non-suicidal Self-Injury (NSSI)** | Self-harm without intent to die | Assess separately; risk marker for future suicide |

| Rating | Description | Medical Threat |

### Risk Stratification & Immediate Action Protocol

| Risk Level | Criteria | Clinical Response |
|------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Low** | No active ideation; no plan; no intent; no history of attempts | Outpatient follow-up; monitor; psychoeducation |
| **Moderate** | Active ideation with vague plan; no intent; may have history of attempts | Safety plan; means restriction; same-week mental health referral; crisis line number |
| **High** | Active ideation + specific plan + expressed intent; recent preparatory acts | Emergency psychiatric evaluation; 1:1 observation; remove lethal means; consider involuntary hold |
| **Imminent** | Current attempt in progress; access to lethal means; psychotic or intoxicated | Activate 911 / emergency services; do not leave patient alone; remove all potential means |

### Lethality Modifiers

**Factors that increase lethality risk:**
- Male sex
- Older age (>45 years)
- Access to firearms
- Substance intoxication at time of attempt
- Social isolation
- Recent discharge from inpatient psychiatric care
- History of prior attempt(s)

**Protective factors:**
- Identified reasons for living (children, pets, faith)
- Engaged in ongoing treatment
- Supportive family/social network
- Future-oriented thinking
- Access to care
