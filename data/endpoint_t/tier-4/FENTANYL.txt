### Forensic Protocol

#### A. Sample Preparation

**Solid samples (powder, tablet):**
1. Homogenize sample by grinding to fine powder.
2. Weigh approximately 10–20 mg sample.
3. Dissolve in 1.0 mL methanol (for GC-MS) or 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile/water (1:1) (for LC-MS/MS).
4. Vortex for 30 seconds.
5. Sonicate for 5 minutes.
6. Centrifuge at 10,000 rpm for 5 minutes.
7. Filter supernatant through 0.22 μm PTFE syringe filter.
8. Dilute as necessary to achieve concentration within calibration range (typically 1–100 ng/mL for LC-MS/MS).

**Biological matrices (blood, urine, plasma):**
1. To 0.5 mL biological fluid, add 50 μL internal standard (f**tanyl-d5, 100 ng/mL).
2. Add 2 mL 0.1 M carbonate buffer (pH 9.5).
3. Extract with 5 mL n-butyl chloride or hexane/ethyl acetate (7:3).
4. Vortex 2 minutes; centrifuge at 3,000 rpm for 10 minutes.
5. Transfer organic layer to clean tube.
6. Evaporate to dryness under nitrogen at 40 °C.
7. Reconstitute in 100 μL mobile phase (LC-MS/MS) or derivatize if required (GC-MS).
8. Inject 2–5 μL.
