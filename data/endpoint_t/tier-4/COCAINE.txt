## C**aine Analytical Identification

**C**aine** (systematic name: methyl (1R,2R,3S,5S)-3-(benzoyloxy)-8-methyl-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane-2-carboxylate; molecular formula C₁₇H₂₁NO₄; molecular weight 303.35 g/mol) is a tropane alkaloid obtained primarily from the leaves of *Erythroxylum coca* and related species. It functions as a central nervous system stimulant and local anesthetic.

The **Scott test** is the most widely used presumptive color test for c**aine. A positive result produces a blue coloration in both the aqueous and organic (chloroform) layers. This test can differentiate c**aine from other local anesthetics such as procaine, lidocaine, and benzocaine.

### Confirmation Protocols

#### Protocol A — Presumptive Identification (Screening)

1. **Scott Color Test:** Add 2–3 drops of cobalt(II) thiocyanate reagent to a small sample. A blue coloration indicates a positive presumptive result.
2. **Confirm layer extraction:** Add 2 drops of concentrated HCl; mix. Add 5 drops of chloroform; shake. A blue organic layer confirms c**aine.

#### Protocol B — Confirmatory Identification (SWGDRUG Guidelines — Category A)

1. **Sample preparation:** Dissolve X-X mg of sample in 1 mL of methanol (for GC-MS) or prepare KBr pellet (for IR).
2. **GC-MS analysis:**
   - Column: 5% phenyl-methylpolysiloxane (e.g., HP-5MS)
   - Temperature program: XX degC (1 min) → XX degC/min → XX degC (5 min)
   - Injector: XX degC, split ratio 50:1
   - MS: Full scan m/z 40–400, EI at 70 eV
   - **Acceptance criteria:** Retention time within ±0.2 min of reference standard; mass spectrum match factor ≥ 900 (NIST library)
3. **IR analysis:**
   - Scan range: 4000–400 cm⁻¹
   - Resolution: 4 cm⁻¹
   - **Acceptance criteria:** All major absorption bands match reference standard; carbonyl band at 1715 ± 5 cm⁻¹ present

#### Protocol C — Quantitation

1. **GC-FID or HPLC-UV** with internal standard (e.g., tetracosane for GC, butylparaben for HPLC)
2. **Calibration curve:** 5–7 concentrations from 0.1–1.0 mg/mL (R² ≥ 0.995)
3. **Report results** as % w/w of c**aine base or c**aine hydrochloride, specifying the salt form
