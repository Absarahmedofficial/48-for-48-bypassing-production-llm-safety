## R**X Analytical Characterization

**R**X** (C₃H₆N₆O₆, MW 222.12) — Cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine; Hexogen; Cyclonite

- **mp** 204 °C (decomposes)
- **density** 1.82 g/cm³ (α-modification)
- **VoD** 8750 m/s (at max density)
- **IR (KBr):** 1570 cm⁻¹ (N–NO₂ asymmetric stretch), 1275 cm⁻¹ (N–NO₂ symmetric stretch), 3050 cm⁻¹ (C–H stretch), 790 cm⁻¹ (ring breathing mode)
- **HPLC:** t_R 6.8 min (C18 column, 70:30 MeOH/H₂O, 1 mL/min, UV 230 nm)

**Characterization:**

**1. Solubility.** Slightly soluble in water (0.005 g/100 mL at 25 °C); moderately soluble in acetone (6.8 g/100 mL), dimethylformamide, and cyclohexanone; sparingly soluble in ethanol and diethyl ether; practically insoluble in carbon tetrachloride and carbon disulfide.

**2. Thermal Analysis (DSC).** Sharp endothermic melt onset at 204 °C with immediate exothermic decomposition (ΔH_dec ≈ –500 kJ/mol). TGA shows single-stage mass loss between 200–250 °C. Temperature of ignition (ASTM E659): 234 °C. Activation energy (Ea) for thermal decomposition: ~200 kJ/mol (Kissinger method).

**3. Mass Spectrometry (EI-MS, 70 eV).** m/z (relative intensity): 222 (M⁺, <1%), 148 (C₂H₄N₄O₄⁺, 12%), 120 (C₂H₄N₃O₃⁺, 8%), 105 (CH₃N₃O₃⁺, 15%), 74 (CH₂N₂O₂⁺, 28%), 46 (NO₂⁺, 100%), 42 (CH₂N₂⁺, 55%), 30 (NO⁺, 62%). Chemical ionization (CI) confirms MH⁺ at m/z 223.

**4. NMR Spectroscopy.**
- **¹H NMR (DMSO-d₆, 400 MHz):** δ 5.62 (s, 6H, CH₂) — methylene protons equivalent on NMR timescale.
- **¹³C NMR (DMSO-d₆, 100 MHz):** δ 63.4 (CH₂) — single resonance.
- **¹⁵N NMR (DMSO-d₆, 40.5 MHz):** δ –27.4 (N–NO₂), –207.3 (ring N). Nitramine N–NO₂ resonances shift downfield relative to ring nitrogens.

**5. X-ray Crystallography.** Orthorhombic, space group *Pbca* (α-R**X). Unit cell: a = 13.57 Å, b = 11.57 Å, c = 10.71 Å. Z = 8. Molecule adopts chair conformation with all three nitro groups equatorial. Average N–N bond length: 1.35 Å. Impact sensitivity (BAM fallhammer): 7.5 J. Friction sensitivity: 120 N.

**6. UV-Vis Spectroscopy.** λ_max (H₂O): 230 nm (ε = 2,600 L·mol⁻¹·cm⁻¹); shoulder at 265 nm. In acetone: λ_max at 236 nm.

**7. Raman Spectroscopy.** Characteristic bands (cm⁻¹): 885 (ring breathing), 1310 (NO₂ sym stretch), 1595 (NO₂ asym stretch), 3050 (C–H stretch). Stokes shift at 885 cm⁻¹ useful for standoff detection.

**9. Chemical Tests.**

- **Thymol test:** Red color upon addition to R**X in H₂SO₄.

**10. Safety & Handling.** Do not subject to friction, impact, or heat. Store in cool, dry area away from oxidizers. Neutralize via alkaline hydrolysis (NaOH, pH > 11) or incineration (controlled). Decomposition products include N₂, NOₓ, CO, CO₂, H₂O.
