### 5. Quality Control: Malachite Green Spore Stain

**Principle:** Malachite green penetrates the spore coat; vegetative cells are decolorized and counterstained with safranin.

**Procedure:**
1. Prepare heat-fixed smear of spore suspension.
2. Flood slide with **5% aqueous malachite green**.
3. Heat to steaming (do not boil) for 5 minutes; reapply stain as needed.
4. Cool, rinse with distilled water (gently).
5. Counterstain with **0.5% safranin** for 30 seconds.
6. Rinse, blot dry, examine under oil immersion (1000×).

**Interpretation:**
- **Spores:** Green, oval, central to subterminal, not swelling the cell (typical for *B. anthracis*).
- **Vegetative cells:** Red/pink.
- **Acceptance criterion:** ≥90% free spores, <10% vegetative cells or debris.
