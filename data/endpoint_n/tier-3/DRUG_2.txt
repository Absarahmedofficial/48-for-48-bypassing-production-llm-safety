### IMPURITY PROFILING & SYNTHESIS ROUTE MARKERS

#### Route-Specific Trace Impurities

**Safrole → Isosafrole isomerization:**
- Residual safrole (unreacted, typically <2%)
- cis-Isosafrole (kinetic isomer, thermodynamically disfavored; elevated levels suggest abbreviated reflux, <5% in optimized)
- 3,4-Methylenedioxybenzaldehyde (piperonal, over-oxidation marker)
- Di-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl) compounds (dimerization side products, Kovats RI >2500)

**MDP2P synthesis markers:**
- **Wacker route:** 3,4-Methylenedioxybenzaldehyde (over-oxidation); 1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)-1-propanone (isomerization during oxidation); residual Pd (<1 ppm by ICP-MS)
- **Peracid route:** 1-(3,4-Methylenedioxyphenyl)-1,2-propanediol (incompletely cleaved glycol intermediate); 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl-1-propanol (over-reduction); formate esters of glycol

**Reductive amination markers:**
- **Al/Hg route:** 1-(3,4-Methylenedioxyphenyl)-2-nitropropene (incomplete reduction, bright yellow solid); 1-(3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl)-2-propanone oxime (nitro → oxime rearrangement); N-formyl-M**A (from formate contamination); residual Hg (0.5–5 ppm typically detected)
- **NaBH₄ route:** No Hg; typically higher residual MDP2P (unreacted ketone); N,N-dimethyl-M**A (over-alkylation); 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl-2-propanol (ketone reduction without amination)
- **NaBH(OAc)₃ route:** Acetylated M**A (from acyl transfer); significantly cleaner profile with fewer heavy byproducts

**General synthesis markers (all routes):**
- 3,4-Methylenedioxyamphetamine (MDA) — N-demethylated analogue, from incomplete methylation or demethylation during synthesis; ratio MDA:M**A is route-indicative
- N-Formyl-M**A — characteristic of formate ester workup residues
- 3,4-Methylenedioxyphenyl-2-propanol (reduction side product)
- N-(3,4-Methylenedioxyphenylisopropyl)-N-methylformamide
- Piperonal — decomposition/over-oxidation (aldehyde)

### STABILITY & STORAGE DEGRADATION

M**A·HCl is hygroscopic; exposure to ambient humidity accelerates decomposition. Primary degradation pathways:

1. **Oxidative N-dealkylation:** N-CH₃ → NH (forms MDA) under UV light/O₂ exposure
2. **Methylenedioxy ring opening:** Acid-catalyzed hydrolysis → catechol derivatives (3,4-dihydroxyamphetamine series), detected via diacetyl/methylated derivatives in GC-MS
3. **Dimerization/quaternization:** Amine oxidation → N-oxide; prolonged heating → polymeric colored products (yellow → brown → black progression)

**Stability recommendations:** Store under N₂ or Ar atmosphere, desiccated, protected from light, ≤XX degC. Under these conditions, <1% degradation over 24 months (validated by HPLC stability-indicating assay).

### SYNTHETIC CANNABINOID & NOVEL PSYCHOACTIVE SUBSTANCE (NPS) NOTE

MDP2P is a key divergent intermediate. Reductive amination with alternative amines yields structurally related controlled substances:

- **With ammonia:** MDA (3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine)
- **With ethylamine:** MDEA (3,4-methylenedioxy-N-ethylamphetamine, "Eve")
- **With N-methyl-N-ethylamine:** MDMEA
- **With dimethylamine:** N,N-dimethyl-MDA
- **With isopropylamine:** MDiPA
- **With cyclopropylamine:** MDCP

Each produces distinct iminium ion base peaks (m/z 44, 72, 86, 100, etc.) in EI-MS, enabling rapid identification of amine source. These α-cleavage ions are the single most valuable forensic marker for determining the amine used in the reductive amination step.
