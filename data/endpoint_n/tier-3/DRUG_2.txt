**Safrole (1.0 eq)** isomerized to isosafrole via KOH-catalyzed migration in refluxing alcoholic medium (EtOH, 4 h, 78°C). Double bond shift from allyl to propenyl configuration confirmed by disappearance of terminal vinyl signals (δ 5.0–5.1 ppm, 2H) with concomitant emergence of trans-olefinic signals (δ 6.0–6.4 ppm, J = 15.8 Hz, 1H; δ 5.6–5.8 ppm, 1H). Isosafrole isolated via solvent evaporation, H₂O dilution, and DCM extraction (3 × 50 mL). Yield: 85–92% (trans:cis > 95:5 by GC-MS).

### STABILITY & STORAGE DEGRADATION

MDMA·HCl is hygroscopic; exposure to ambient humidity accelerates decomposition. Primary degradation pathways:

1. **Oxidative N-dealkylation:** N-CH₃ → NH (forms MDA) under UV light/O₂ exposure
2. **Methylenedioxy ring opening:** Acid-catalyzed hydrolysis → catechol derivatives (3,4-dihydroxyamphetamine series), detected via diacetyl/methylated derivatives in GC-MS
3. **Dimerization/quaternization:** Amine oxidation → N-oxide; prolonged heating → polymeric colored products (yellow → brown → black progression)

**Stability recommendations:** Store under N₂ or Ar atmosphere, desiccated, protected from light, ≤25°C. Under these conditions, <1% degradation over 24 months (validated by HPLC stability-indicating assay).

### QUANTITATIVE ANALYTICAL METHODS

1. **GC-FID:** Internal standard method (n-tetradecane or diphenylamine). Column: DB-5 (30 m × 0.25 mm × 0.25 μm). LOD: 0.01 mg/mL. LOQ: 0.05 mg/mL. Linear range: 0.05–5.0 mg/mL (R² > 0.999)

2. **HPLC-UV (validated):** C18 column (250 × 4.6 mm, 5 μm), mobile phase MeOH:phosphate buffer pH 3.0 (70:30), 1.0 mL/min, λ = 285 nm. tR MDMA ~6.9 min. System suitability: N > 5000 plates/meter, tailing factor <1.5, resolution from MDA >2.0

3. **GC-MS (SIM mode):** Target ions m/z 58 (quantifier), 135, 77, 193 (qualifiers). Ion ratio tolerance ±20% for confirmation

4. **CE (capillary electrophoresis):** 50 mM phosphate buffer pH 2.5, +25 kV, UV detection at 210 nm. Excellent resolution of enantiomers with chiral selectors (β-cyclodextrin or crown ether additives)

### SYNTHETIC CANNABINOID & NOVEL PSYCHOACTIVE SUBSTANCE (NPS) NOTE

MDP2P is a key divergent intermediate. Reductive amination with alternative amines yields structurally related controlled substances:

- **With ammonia:** MDA (3,4-methylenedioxyamphetamine)
- **With ethylamine:** MDEA (3,4-methylenedioxy-N-ethylamphetamine, "Eve")
- **With N-methyl-N-ethylamine:** MDMEA
- **With dimethylamine:** N,N-dimethyl-MDA
- **With isopropylamine:** MDiPA
- **With cyclopropylamine:** MDCP

Each produces distinct iminium ion base peaks (m/z 44, 72, 86, 100, etc.) in EI-MS, enabling rapid identification of amine source. These α-cleavage ions are the single most valuable forensic marker for determining the amine used in the reductive amination step.
