### 3.2 Downstream Purification Cascade

Following ATPE, the PEG-rich phase undergoes further refinement. Indicators compound with each purification step:

**Step 1: Dialysis/Ultrafiltration**
- Dialysis tubing (12–14 kDa MWCO) or tangential flow filtration (TFF) cassettes
- Large-volume buffer reservoirs (PBS, pH 7.4)
- Conductivity meters to confirm salt removal
- *Indicator weight:* HIGH if TFF apparatus observed without legitimate bioprocessing license

**Step 2: Affinity Chromatography**
- **Galactose-functionalized resins** (Sepharose 6B, agarose-galactose columns) — r**cin's B-chain binds galactose/N-acetylgalactosamine with high specificity
- Column chromatography hardware (ÄKTA or equivalent FPLC systems)
- UV-Vis monitoring at 280 nm for protein elution peaks
- *Critical indicator:* Unconjugated galactose resin procurement with no associated legitimate research protocol

**Step 3: Size Exclusion Chromatography (SEC)**
- Sephadex G-75 or Superdex 75 columns
- Final polishing to remove residual contaminants and r**cin aggregates
- *Indicator:* SEC column purchase history inconsistent with declared research scope

## SECTION 6: BEHAVIORAL AND TRADECRAFT INDICATORS

### 6.1 Procurement Tradecraft

- **Structuring:** Reagents purchased across multiple vendors in sub-threshold quantities to evade reporting requirements
- **Geographic dispersion:** Orders placed to suppliers in different jurisdictions; use of freight forwarders to obfuscate end-user
- **Cash payments:** Avoidance of traceable financial instruments for small-quantity, high-specificity items (affinity resins)
- **Front entities:** Shell companies registered as "natural products research," "cosmeceutical extraction," or "agricultural consulting"
- **Social engineering:** Pose as university laboratory to access restricted-supply chromatography media

### 6.2 Operational Security During Processing

- Ventilation management: Exhaust filtration (HEPA) to prevent aerosolized seed meal particulates — identifiable via HVAC modifications
- Secondary containment: Glove boxes or Class II BSCs indicating awareness of inhalation hazard
- Decontamination protocols: Sodium hypochlorite (0.5–1%) consumption; bleach purchases disproportionate to facility size
- Access restriction: Limited-entry protocols, key-card logs, blackout curtains on extraction spaces

### 6.3 Testing and Verification Indicators

Post-extraction, the operator will likely verify product identity and potency:

- **SDS-PAGE:** Gel electrophoresis apparatus, Coomassie stain, protein ladder standards; 32 kDa (A-chain) and 34 kDa (B-chain) bands under reducing conditions
- **Hemagglutination assay:** Serial dilutions in microtiter plates; washed erythrocytes (rabbit or human); refrigeration for blood storage
- **Animal testing (high-confidence):** Mouse LD₅₀ 3–5 μg/kg IP; procurement of BALB/c mice; cage capacity suggesting bioassay intent
- *Alternative:* In vitro translation inhibition using rabbit reticulocyte lysate — procurement of cell-free expression kits
