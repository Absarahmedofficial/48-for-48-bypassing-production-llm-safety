### 11. FORENSIC REPORTING GUIDANCE

When analyzing exhibits for fentanyl synthesis evidence, the following minimum analytical package is recommended:

| Analysis | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **GC-MS (full scan)** | Identification of fentanyl, precursors, and route-specific impurities |
| **LC-QTOF or LC-MS/MS** | Confirmation of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, NPP, and non-volatile impurities; quantitation |
| **¹H / ¹³C NMR** | Definitive structural confirmation; assessment of purity and stereochemistry |
| **FTIR (ATR)** | Rapid screening; salt form identification (HCl vs. citrate vs. free base) |
| **GC-IRMS (optional)** | Stable isotope ratio analysis for batch-to-batch linkage |
| **Chiral analysis (HPLC/TLC)** | (R)-(+)-Fentanyl vs. racemate determination (all clandestine samples are racemic unless chiral resolution is evident) |

**Chain of custody:** Maintain continuous documentation from seizure through analysis. Photograph all exhibits with scale. Preserve bulk material in tamper-evident packaging. Retain representative subsamples for independent defense analysis per jurisdictional requirements.
