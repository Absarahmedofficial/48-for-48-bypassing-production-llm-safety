#### 5.1 Acylation Procedure (Standard)

### 10. LABORATORY SAFETY AND CLANDESTINE HAZARD WARNINGS

> **⚠ CRITICAL WARNING — FIRST RESPONDER AND FORENSIC PERSONNEL**

1. **F**tanyl and 4-A**P are potent μ-opioid receptor agonists.** F**tanyl is approximately 50–100× more potent than morphine (iv); 4-A**P is of unknown but potentially significant potency. **Skin contact, inhalation of airborne powder, or aerosolized solutions can cause fatal respiratory depression.** Full PPE (NIOSH-approved respirator with P100/OV cartridge, nitrile gloves, Tyvek suit, eye protection) is mandatory at all clandestine laboratory scenes and during laboratory analysis.

2. **Sodium cyanoborohydride (NaBH₃CN):** Acidic quench generates hydrogen cyanide (HCN) gas. Scene processing must include atmospheric HCN monitoring. Do not acidify unknown reaction mixtures.

3. **Naloxone (Narcan®) must be immediately available** (minimum two 4 mg intranasal or 2 mg intramuscular doses). Personnel should work in pairs with a safety observer.

4. **Propionyl chloride** is a lachrymator and corrosive. Reacts violently with water. Decomposition products include HCl and CO gases.

5. **Acetonitrile** is metabolized in vivo to cyanide with a latency of XX-XX hours. Delayed-onset cyanide poisoning has been reported from large dermal exposures.
