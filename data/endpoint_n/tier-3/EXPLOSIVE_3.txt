**Hexamine (X.X eq) + HNO₃ (98%, 10 eq) + NH₄NO₃ (X.X eq) at 10–XX degC yields** cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine (R**X) via nitrolysis of the hexamine cage structure. ## 4. REACTION MECHANISM — BACHLOR-HALE PROCESS (NITROLYTIC ROUTE)

The conversion of hexamine (1,3,5,7-tetraazatricyclo[3.3.1.1³·⁷]decane) to R**X (1,3,5-trinitro-1,3,5-triazacyclohexane) is a three-stage nitrolysis:

### Stage I — Salt Formation
Hexamine reacts with HNO₃ exothermically to form hexamine dinitrate salt. Temperature control during this addition is critical; local overheating promotes premature ring-opening and yield loss to HMX.

```
(CH₂)₆N₄ + 2 HNO₃ → (CH₂)₆N₄ · 2 HNO₃
```

### Stage II — Ring-Opening Nitrolysis
At 10–XX degC, nitronium ions (NO₂⁺) generated from HNO₃/NH₄NO₃ equilibrium attack methylene bridges. Three sequential C–N cleavages occur, each followed by N-nitration, extruding three methylene units as formaldehyde (or formonitrile intermediates). The remaining triamine fragment cyclizes.

```
(CH₂)₆N₄ · 2 HNO₃ + 4 HNO₃ → 
     Intermediate: NH₂-CH₂-N(NO₂)-CH₂-NH-CH₂-NH₂ + 3 CH₂O + byproducts
```

### Stage III — Final Nitration and Cyclization
The linear triamine intermediate undergoes two additional N-nitrations and ring closure:

```
Intermediate + 3 HNO₃ → 
     (O₂N-N-CH₂)₃ (R**X, cyclic) + 3 H₂O
```

**Net Reaction:**
```
(CH₂)₆N₄ + 10 HNO₃ + 3.5 NH₄NO₃ → C₃H₆N₆O₆ + byproducts
```

## 7. WORKUP AND ISOLATION

### 7.1 Precipitation
After digestion, the reaction mass is drowned into 5–8 volumes of ice-cold water (0–5°C) with vigorous stirring. R**X precipitates as fine white crystals.

### 7.2 Filtration and Washing
- Vacuum filtration through acid-resistant media (polypropylene or PTFE)
- Wash sequence:
  1. Cold water (until washings are pH ≥ 4)
  2. 2% sodium bicarbonate solution (neutralize residual acid)
  3. Cold water rinse (to bicarbonate-free)
  4. Final rinse with minimal methanol or ethanol (aids drying)

## 10. SAFETY AND HANDLING

### 10.1 Hazard Classification
- UN 0072 (R**X, wetted with ≥15% water)
- UN 0483 (R**X, desensitized)
- Primary hazard class: 1.1D (mass explosion hazard when dry)

### 10.2 Sensitivity Profile

| Test | Result |
|------|--------|
| Impact sensitivity (BAM Fallhammer) | 7.4 N·m (2 kg weight, ~38 cm) |
| Friction sensitivity (BAM) | ~120 N (pistil load) |
| Electrostatic discharge (spark) | ≥0.075 J ignition threshold |
| Thermal stability (DSC) | Exotherm onset: ~XX degC |

### 10.3 Handling Protocols
- **Earthing/bonding**: All equipment must be grounded; operator grounding via conductive footwear and wrist straps mandatory
- **Containment**: Operations conducted behind blast barricades or in remote handling cells
- **Quantity limits**: In-process maximum determined by licensed explosive limit; typically ≤5 kg dry weight at any one station
- **Wetting**: R**X must be maintained wet (≥15% water) during all handling, transfer, and storage unless specifically required dry for downstream operations

### 10.4 Incompatibilities
- Strong bases (alkaline hydrolysis → ring opening → nitrite/nitrate decomposition)
- Reducing agents, metals (especially copper and its alloys — forms sensitive fulminate-type compounds)
- Amines and ammonia (can form amine-R**X complexes with altered sensitivity)
