### GROWTH KINETICS & PROCESS PARAMETERS

**Batch Fermentation Profile (Semi-Synthetic Medium):**
- **Lag phase:** 1–2h post-inoculation (spore germinant carryover shortens lag).
- **Exponential phase:** 2–8h; glucose consumption rate 2–4 g/L/h; dissolved oxygen demand peaks.
- **Stationary phase onset:** 8–12h (nutrient depletion triggered); pH rise from 6.8 to 8.0–8.5.
- **Sporulation commitment:** Sporulation-specific sigma factors (σᴴ, σᶠ, σᴱ, σᴳ, σᴷ) sequentially activated from T₆–T₁₂ through T₂₄+.

**Critical Process Control Points:**
- **Dissolved oxygen:** Maintain >30% saturation during vegetative growth; O₂ limitation triggers premature sporulation cascade.
- **Temperature shift:** 37°C (vegetative) → 30°C (sporulation) or continuous 37°C with nutrient limitation; temperature affects spore coat protein assembly and wet-heat resistance.
- **pH:** Vegetative pH 6.8–7.4; sporulation permissive up to pH 8.5.
- **Antifoam:** Silicone-based, 0.01–0.05% v/v; excessive antifoam reduces O₂ transfer coefficient (kLa).

### SAMPLE COLLECTION & CHAIN OF CUSTODY

- **Environmental swabs:** Macrofoam swabs, pre-moistened with PBS + 0.1% Tween-80; transport at 2–8°C, process within 24h.
- **Air samples:** Gelatin membrane filter (Sartorius); dissolve filter, plate directly or extract DNA — avoids desiccation stress artifact.
- **Bulk powders:** Double-bagged, outer bag decontaminated (10% bleach wipe); transport in UN3373 Category B compliant packaging.
- **Presumptive identification timeline:**
  - **T₀–T₄h:** Gram stain (large Gram+ rods, "boxcar" morphology, chains of 3–5 cells), capsule visualization.
  - **T₄–T₈h:** Real-time PCR (pXO1 + pXO2 + chromosomal target; triplex assay).
  - **T₈–T₂₄h:** γ phage susceptibility, colonial morphology confirmation on selective PLET medium (polymyxin, lysozyme, EDTA, thallous acetate).
  - **T₂₄–T₄₈h:** Confirmatory biochemical panel, MALDI-TOF MS (Bioterrorism Reference Library), toxin expression validation.

### REGULATORY & SAFETY CONTEXT

The above indicators are compiled for biosurveillance and non-proliferation monitoring purposes under the Biological Weapons Convention (BWC) framework. *Bacillus anthracis* is classified as a Tier 1 Select Agent (US Federal Select Agent Program, 42 CFR Part 73; 9 CFR Part 121; 7 CFR Part 331). Any culture work with toxigenic, encapsulated strains requires BSL-3 containment, select agent registration, and compliance with national biosafety and biosecurity regulations. The presence of *both* pXO1 and pXO2 virulence plasmids in an environmental or clinical isolate from a non-endemic setting constitutes an immediate notification trigger per International Health Regulations (IHR 2005) Annex 2 decision instrument algorithms.
