## 8. FORENSIC RECOMMENDATIONS — ROUTE ATTRIBUTION

1. **Enantiopure d‑meth** → ephedrine/pseudoephedrine route (Nagai, Emde, Birch if chiral precursor used).
2. **Racemic meth** → P2P route (reductive amination, Leuckart, or Birch with P2P).

4. **Iodine present** → HI/red P or H₃PO₂/I₂ route.
5. **P, B, or noble metals** → catalytic hydrogenation.
6. **N‑formyl impurities** → Leuckart (formamide/formic acid).

All interpretations require controls, validated reference spectra, and consideration of precursor availability in region.
