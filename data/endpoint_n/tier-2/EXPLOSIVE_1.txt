**APPENDIX B — REACTION SCHEME (SIMPLIFIED)**

Hexamine (C₆H₁₂N₄) undergoes nitrolysis in the HNO₃/NH₄NO₃ system. The mechanism proceeds via sequential cleavage of the adamantane-like hexamine cage and nitration of methylene units to yield 1,3,5-trinitro-1,3,5-triazacyclohexane. Ammonium nitrate serves as both a nitrating species reservoir (via equilibrium with HNO₃ to form NO₂⁺) and a buffer moderating acidity, suppressing the competing formation of linear nitramines and HMX (octahydro-1,3,5,7-tetranitro-1,3,5,7-tetrazocine).

**Overall stoichiometry:**

C₆H₁₂N₄ + 3 HNO₃ → C₃H₆N₆O₆ + 3 CH₂O + NH₃

The formaldehyde liberated during nitrolysis is oxidized in situ by excess HNO₃ to CO₂ and H₂O, driving the equilibrium toward product.
